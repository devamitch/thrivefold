Thrivefold is a wellbeing companion for daily check-ins, movement, meals, water and personal routines. Use the app without an account; selected profile and progress details can sync when you sign in.

DAILY WELLBEING
• Check in on your mood, energy, stress and sleep.
• Build routines with quests, reminders and progress views.
• Keep private journal, cycle and health logs on your device by default.

MOVEMENT
• Follow guided home workouts and movement sessions.
• With camera permission, count repetitions and receive movement cues on your device. Camera frames are processed in memory and are not saved by this feature.
• Track steps from your phone or connected sources when you grant permission.

FOOD AND ACTIVITY
• Log meals and water.
• Review food and nutrition information from the app's food data.
• Ask for an optional AI meal estimate; the selected photo is sent for processing when you request it.
• Connect Health Connect for supported activity and health records, with permission for each data type.

YOUR DATA
• No account is required for local wellbeing features.
• Signing in can sync selected profile and progress details. Weekly summaries and cloud AI are separate optional features.
• Usage analytics and crash reports are off by default and can be enabled in Privacy settings.
• Export local app data or erase it from the device in the app settings.

Thrivefold supports general wellbeing and healthy habits. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Cycle predictions and nutrition estimates are approximate. Consult a qualified clinician with health concerns or before starting a new exercise programme.
