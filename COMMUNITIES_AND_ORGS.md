# Thrivefold Segmented Communities & Organization Portals

## 1. Segmented Private Affinity Circles

Thrivefold replaces open, chaotic social media timelines with private, intentional circles structured by verified experience and life phase.

### Dedicated Safe Spaces:
1. **Women's Health Circles**:
   - `PCOS & Endometriosis Support`: Shared symptom management, dietary tips, and hormonal resilience.
   - `Matrescence & Postpartum Care`: Mental health support for new mothers and postpartum recovery.
   - `Perimenopause & Beyond`: Hormonal shifts, sleep remedies, and joint vitality.
2. **Men's Health Circles**:
   - `Fatherhood & Daily Work-Life Balance`: Managing stress, presence, and family wellbeing.
   - `Strength, Longevity & Recovery`: Cardiovascular conditioning, sleep optimization, and active aging.
3. **Pride & LGBTQ+ Affinity Circles**:
   - `Affirming Health Navigation`: Safe physician directories and gender-affirming healthcare guidance.
   - `Chosen Family & Mental Haven`: Compassionate peer check-ins and mutual affirmation.

---

## 2. Policy-Governed Community Access
- **18+ Age Assurance**: Community discovery is locked behind adult verification.
- **Trial User Isolation**: Under-25 pilot participants in introductory trials are strictly barred from the community directory (`COMMUNITY_RESTRICTED_DURING_TRIAL`) to protect existing community safety.
- **Chip & Prompt Moderation**: Posts are composed via curated topic prompts, eliminating spam, solicitation, and harassment.

---

## 3. Organization Verification Portal (`/organizations`)
Designed for universities, corporate wellness programs, and clinical trial partners:
- **Badge Verification**: Issues non-identifying cryptographic credential badges to members (e.g. `Verified University Member`, `Clinical Study Cohort`).
- **Anonymized Aggregated Reports**: Organizations never see individual user health entries, letters, or journals—only macro-level cohort wellness trends (e.g., 78% average sleep goal achievement).
