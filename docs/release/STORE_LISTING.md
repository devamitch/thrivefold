```text
Thrivefold is your calm personal companion for mindful movement, bodyweight biomechanics, daily habits, and vitality.

Designed to help you build sustainable daily health without noisy social feeds or clinical pressure, Thrivefold combines on-device pose tracking with guided movement sessions, wellness quests, and holistic calendar planning.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✨ KEY FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🤸 ON-DEVICE POSE COACHING & FORM FEEDBACK
• Real-Time Landmarks: Utilizes privacy-preserving on-device camera computer vision to observe posture alignment and movement flow.
• Zero Cloud Video: All camera frames are processed exclusively in volatile device memory and never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
• Guided Form Guides: Visual and gesture cues guide your movement pacing and rest transitions.

🌿 MINDFUL TRAINING & CALM SESSIONS
• Bodyweight Basics: Core strength, mobility flows, and low-impact cardio routines designed for all fitness levels.
• Personalized Movement: Choose between home routines, steady-pace mobility, and posture resets.
• Audio & Motion Guidance: Gentle audio chimes and clear visual guides keep you focused on your breath and form.

📅 HOLISTIC CALENDAR & DAILY QUESTS
• Daily Check-ins: Quick 1-minute reflection on energy, mood, and movement intentions.
• Flow Quests: Bite-sized daily actions that reward consistent, mindful habits with experience points (XP).
• Rhythm Strip: Visual timeline of past sessions, streaks, and personal milestones.

🛡️ THE ANVAYKOSH ECOSYSTEM & SOVEREIGN PRIVACY
• Anvay ID 1-Tap Sign-In: Seamless, federated access across all flagship Amit Dev Labs ecosystem apps.
• Local-First & Offline Ready: All workouts, logs, and habit records function fully offline in Airplane Mode.
• Zero Telemetry: No third-party ad trackers, covert device fingerprinting, or invasive analytics.
• Hardware Encrypted Vault: Your health metrics stay encrypted on your device and are never shared without explicit consent.
```
